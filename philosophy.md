# YACL Philosophy

- **Name = Behavior**: names are operational commands, not labels.
- **Structure first**: prefer structural refactor over adding flags.
- **Contract & Assertions**: outputs are validated against explicit contracts.
- **Human in the loop**: structure controls output; humans do the final polish.
